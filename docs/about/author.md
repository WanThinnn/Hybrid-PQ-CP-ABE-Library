# Author

## Lai Quan Thien

**Lai Quan Thien**, known online as **WanThinnn** and **Elio**, is a cybersecurity
engineer with a background in Information Security at the University of
Information Technology, VNU-HCM. His work spans SOC monitoring, threat detection,
incident response, security automation and applied cryptography. His projects
include policy-based encrypted storage and database protection using CP-ABE and
AES-GCM. [Personal portfolio](https://wanthinnn.github.io/)

## Cyberfortress Labs

He also works on the **Intelligent SOC Ecosystem** presented by Cyberfortress
Labs, a graduation-thesis project connecting SIEM, SOAR, threat intelligence and
AI-assisted analysis. The ecosystem brings together Elastic Stack, Wazuh,
Suricata, Zeek, MISP, IntelOwl, DFIR-IRIS and n8n, with SmartXDR providing analysis
and decision support across the monitoring and response pipeline.
[Cyberfortress Labs](https://cyberfortress-labs.github.io/)
